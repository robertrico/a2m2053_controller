# M1: mechanism status reads

Date: 2026-10-10. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-07-m0-m3-mechanism-bringup.md`, Task 10.
Firmware: branch `m1-console` at `c5e1deb` (Tasks 6 and 7), flashed over SWD,
Verified OK. Console on `/dev/cu.usbmodem114401`, driven by a small script
(pyserial is not installed).

## Step 1: drive identification

`oe on`, `enbl on`, `status`, no disk:

```
STATUS DIRTN=0 CSTIN=1 STEP=1 WRPROT=0 MOTORON=1 TK0=0 SWITCHED=0 TACH=0 RDDATA0=0 RDDATA1=0 SUPERDRIVE=0 REG_B=0 SIDES=1 READY=1 DRVIN=0 REVISED=1
```

DRVIN=0, SIDES=1, REVISED=1, SUPERDRIVE=0 and MOTORON=1 all match the
expected 800K mechanism. The mechanism stayed silent through `oe on` and
`enbl on`.

## Step 2: disk in / out, write-protect tab

| State | CSTIN | WRPROT | TK0 | Capture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No disk | 1 | 0 | 0 | `docs/captures/m1/2026-10-10_status_no-disk.txt` |
| Disk in, write-enabled (hole covered) | 0 | 1 | 1 | `..._status_disk_write-enabled.txt` |
| Disk in, protected (hole open) | 0 | 0 | 1 | `..._status_disk_protected.txt` |

WRPROT is active low as documented: 0 = protected. It also reads 0 with no
disk. The first insert was labeled "protected" on the bench but the tab was
the other way round; flipping it confirmed the polarity.

The mechanism spun the motor for about half a second by itself when the disk
went in. The console was idle and no command had been sent.

TK0 read 0 before the first insert and 1 with a disk in. The head may have
moved during the insert cycle. M2's recal will show where it is.

TACH read 0 once and 1 once with the motor off. That is just the level the
tach line rests at.

## Step 3: settle time (CA0 change to RD valid)

Scope CH1 on CN104 pin 2 (CA0), CH2 on CN104 pin 16 (RD), 10x probes, DC.
`status 12` (SIDES = 1) and `status 14` (DRVIN = 0) differ only in CA0.

| Edge | Delay (50 % to 50 %) | Edge time | Capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA0 rise -> RD fall | 27 ns (scope measurement) | CA0 rise 6.8 ns, RD fall 8.2 ns | `2026-10-10_settle_ca0-rise_rd-fall_2us.png` |
| CA0 fall -> RD rise | about 36 ns (read off the screen at 100 ns/div; waveform download returned no samples) | RD rise about 10 ns | `2026-10-10_settle_ca0-fall_rd-rise_100ns.png` |

RD is actively driven both ways; the rise isn't a slow pull-up edge. Settle
is well under the 5 us threshold, so `settle` stays at its 10 us default. M4
can drop it to 1 us, still more than 25 times the measured delay.

## Surprises

- The mechanism spins briefly on disk insert without being commanded.
- TK0 changed from 0 to 1 across the disk insert.
