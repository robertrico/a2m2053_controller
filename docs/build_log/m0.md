# M0: bench rig, power, boot safety

Date: 2026-10-10. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-07-m0-m3-mechanism-bringup.md`, Task 9.

## Connector orientation (continuity, unpowered)

CN104 / mechanism CN1 orientation confirmed on a dead-board drive:

- Pin 1 to GND: beeps on the test cable, the mechanism board and the GND test point.
- Pin 8 to the LSTRB test point: beeps through connector and test cable.
- Pin 11 to the 5VF test point: beeps.
- No shorts between +5V, +12V and GND.

## Wiring as built

U1 (mechanism RD in) and U2 (mechanism outputs) only. GPIO map per
`firmware/src/pins.h` and `docs/pin_budget.md`.

- Shifter orientation as planned: Pico on A (VCCA = 3V3), mechanism on B (VCCB = +5V).
  DIR and /OE are referenced to VCCA, so they stay in the 3V3 domain.
- B-side pulls: /ENBL 10k to +5V, /WRTGATE 10k to +5V, LSTRB 10k to GND,
  RD 10k to +5V. U2 /OE 10k to 3V3.
- Pin 20 (/PWM) tied to +5V. Pin 9 N/C.

RD (pin 16) to +5V reads 3.3k with the mechanism connected, versus 10k for our
resistor alone. The parallel path works out to about 4.9k, likely a pull-up on
the mechanism. Rechecked with the mechanism unplugged: reading is as expected
for our 10k alone, so the extra path is on the mechanism.

## Step 2: mechanism power, Pico unplugged

Supply limits: +5V 1.0 A, +12V 1.5 A. No disk.

| Measurement | Value |
|---|---|
| +5V idle current | 0.01 A (supply readout, 10 mA resolution) |
| +12V idle current | 0.00 A (supply readout) |
| CN104 pin 11 | 4.90 V |
| CN104 pin 13 | 11.94 V |
| Mechanism | Silent. No spin, step or eject. |

/ENBL is held high by its pull-up, so the mechanism stays in standby. Idle
current is low and the 12V rail is unloaded with the motors off.

## Step 3: flash

Mechanism supply off. Debug Probe (CMSIS-DAP fw 2.0.0) over SWD at 5 MHz,
Raspberry Pi OpenOCD. Target: RP2350 rev 2, W25Q32 flash (4 MiB).
`make test` passes (crc32, check_wrtgate). `make flash`: Verified OK.

Console on the Pico's USB CDC port (`/dev/cu.usbmodem114401` this session;
`114202` is the probe's UART). Enter returns `OK a2m_bringup (scaffold)`.

## Step 4: boot-safety scope check

Capture and setup: `docs/captures/m0/2026-10-10_boot-safety-reset.{png,md}`.
CH1 GP21 (U2 /OE), CH2 LSTRB (pin 8), CH3 CA1 (pin 4). Mechanism supply on.
Reset by `make reset` (SWD `reset run`), confirmed by USB re-enumeration.

| Run | Trigger | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CH1 falling, 1.52 V | No trigger: GP21 stayed high through reset and boot |
| 2 | CH2 rising, 1.52 V | No trigger: no LSTRB pulse |
| 3 | CH3 rising, 1.52 V | No trigger: no CA1 pulse |

Static levels: GP21 3.21 V mean, LSTRB 0 V, CA1 0 V. Mechanism silent
throughout. **Pass.**

Limits of this test:
- 10 MSa/s sampling (100 ns per sample). A glitch shorter than about 100 ns
  could be missed. The 50 ms/div window was chosen to cover the whole boot.
- GP21 carries about 400 mV p-p of noise at the high level (3.21 V mean,
  3.44 V max). Well clear of the /OE threshold, but worth a look later.

## Step 4b: Pico power cycle with the mechanism powered

Captures and setup: `docs/captures/m0/2026-10-10_usb-power-cycle.md`.
Trigger on LSTRB rising, Pico USB unplugged and replugged.

| Event | LSTRB | /ENBL | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unplug (twice) | Driven to 5 V for about 1.5 ms when 3V3 sags to about 0.8 V. CA1 also goes to 5 V. | Dips to 3.64 V minimum | Mechanism silent |
| Replug | Driven to 5 V for about 1.3 us when 3V3 passes about 1 V | 4.76 V minimum | Mechanism silent |

**Finding:** U2 /OE does not keep the 8T245 quiet while VCCA is between 0 V
and its 1.65 V minimum. The datasheet guarantees isolation only with VCCA at
0 V. In that range U2 drives its B outputs high briefly. Each Pico power edge
sends a full-swing LSTRB strobe to the mechanism.

The mechanism ignores the strobe because /ENBL stays high, so the drive is
never selected. Margin on the unplug is thin: 3.64 V against 3.5 V for a
CMOS VIH at 5 V. The CX23077A input type is unknown.

**Bench rule:** switch the mechanism supply off before unplugging or
replugging the Pico. Power-up order: Pico first, then the mechanism.
Power-down: mechanism first, then the Pico.

**Design note:** on the final board, 3V3 comes from the host +5V, so both
rails rise and fall together and the case is milder. A 3V3 supervisor
holding U2 and U4 off below a valid VCCA was considered and dropped at M9:
at worst the mechanism steps or blips at power-on, as stock drives do
(`m9.md`).

## Surprises

- RD has a second pull-up of about 4.9k, on the mechanism (see above).
- U2 drives LSTRB high on each Pico power edge. /ENBL holds the mechanism off (Step 4b).
