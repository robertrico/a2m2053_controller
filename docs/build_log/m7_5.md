# M7.5: host power

2026-10-15. Goal (parent spec sec. 4.2): retire the bench supply. Our board
and the mechanism run on the IIe rails through J1.

## What changed

- Bench supply leads off the board. J1.11 (+5 V) feeds the board +5 net
  (U1-U5 VCCB, pull-ups, CN104 +5). J1.13 and J1.15 (+12 V) feed CN104 +12.
  J1.9 (-12 V) only passes through to J3.
- The Pico stays on USB; its VSYS/VBUS are not tied to the IIe +5.
- Mechanism on the J2 breakout (off-the-shelf IDC board), no longer the old
  CN104 tap.
- Power order: Pico USB, then IIe. Shutdown: IIe, then Pico USB.

## What I measured

| Check | Result |

| Stage 1 (logic only), board +5 / +12 | 4.54 V / 12.2 V |
| Stage 2, CN104.11 with the mechanism running | 4.54 V |

| SPCOMPARE, writable disk | ST0 `F8 40 06 00`, ST3/ST5/ST1 as M7, RB0-2 sums 56491 / 0 / 15100, RB1600 `$2D`: matches the M7 table |

## Open

- +5 sat at 4.54 V, below the ERS 4.75 V minimum. Traced in M8 the same
  day: the IIe slot gives 5.34 V and thin jumper wire on the +5 path lost
  the rest. Replaced with 24 AWG; with the UniDisk chained behind us the
  feed reads 4.7 V (`m8.md`).
- The full-disk read scan, the write-protected / no-disk SPCOMPARE states
  and eject were run on IIe power in M8 and match M7 (`m8.md`, "Clean-boot
  check" and "Eject button").
